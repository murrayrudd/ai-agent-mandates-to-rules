A standing mandate lets the agent choose A, B, or DECLINE and act directly. The standing agent reports disposition ACT and issue NONE. An agent with referral authority can instead report REFER with issue CONTROL or OTHER. It must record its provisional purchase choice before each referral. The operator can select either offer or decline the current purchase. A ruling on one purchase expires with it. A newly authorized standing clarification applies from the next purchase in its scope. Each new purchase shows the instructions actually in force.
